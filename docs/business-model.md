# Business model: open questions

No commission, exclusivity, margins or representation agreement is assumed. Keep private negotiations in Drive, not Git.

Hypotheses to test: traditional dealer orders, made-to-order titanium frames, referral relationships and custom-build partnerships. Compare inventory risk, dealer needs, lead times, warranty responsibilities, ordering process and customer ownership with real feedback.

Clarify product specifications and availability first. When genuine opportunities emerge, discuss quotation ownership, payment and fulfillment responsibilities, commission attribution/basis/timing, returns and warranty handling with Equator. Do not calculate commission using an invented rate. Estimated and confirmed order values and commissions have separate tracker columns; confirmed entries require a confirmation source. Never sum different currencies as if equivalent.
