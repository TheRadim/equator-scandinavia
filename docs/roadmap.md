# Roadmap


Project start: October 12, 2026.

Primary geographic priority:
1. Copenhagen and Greater Copenhagen.
2. Rest of Denmark.
3. Sweden and Norway later, if the Danish pilot shows promise.

The first three months are exploratory.

Do not treat the targets as guaranteed results.

## WEEK 2 — OCTOBER 19–25
### Initial market validation

Estimated commitment: 6–10 hours.

Tasks:

1. Research the five highest-priority Danish shops.
2. Send five personalized introductory emails after my approval.
3. Identify five additional relevant shops.
4. Contact another three to five businesses after my approval.
5. Research custom-build and made-to-order dealer models.
6. Track responses and objections.
7. Identify common questions about pricing, delivery, and products.
8. Evaluate whether shops prefer:
   - Traditional dealer purchases.
   - Made-to-order frames.
   - Referral partnerships.
   - Custom bicycle builds.
9. Prepare a short market feedback summary.

Initial planning targets:
- 50–100 businesses discovered.
- 10–15 qualified leads.
- 8–10 personalized outreach messages.
- 1–3 meaningful conversations.
- 0–2 potentially interested partners.

These are exploratory targets, not performance commitments.

## WEEK 3 — OCTOBER 26–NOVEMBER 1
### Evaluation and refinement

Estimated commitment: 6–10 hours.

Tasks:

1. Follow up with relevant businesses.
2. Arrange calls or visits with interested shops.
3. Analyze responses and objections.
4. Compare potential distribution models.
5. Identify missing sales materials.
6. Evaluate initial pricing observations.
7. Prepare a progress update for Equator.
8. Arrange a second discussion with Equator contacts.
9. Agree on November priorities.
10. If real sales opportunities are developing, begin clarifying
    commission attribution and commercial responsibilities.

Deliverables:
- Initial market validation report.
- Updated partner shortlist.
- Recommended distribution approach.
- November action plan.

## NOVEMBER 2026 — WEEKS 4–7
### Relationship development

Objectives:

1. Develop 3–5 meaningful relationships with relevant businesses.
2. Continue qualifying potential partners.
3. Explore dealer pricing and ordering requirements.
4. Create essential English-language product materials if needed.
5. Develop initial international brand positioning.
6. Identify opportunities for product photography and storytelling.
7. Explore possible demonstrations or test bikes.
8. Clarify the practical sales process with Equator.
9. Discuss commission structure when commercially relevant.
10. Document what is working and what is not.

Do not build unnecessary marketing materials before validating demand.

## DECEMBER 2026 — WEEKS 8–11
### Commercial opportunities and brand development

Objectives:

1. Advance conversations with interested partners.
2. Support requests for product information or quotations.
3. Explore potential pilot orders.
4. Improve product presentation based on feedback.
5. Develop useful marketing assets.
6. Explore consumer-facing storytelling.
7. Evaluate whether a demonstration frame would help.
8. Document potential commercial opportunities.
9. Review progress with Equator.

A first sale would be welcome but is not required for the
project to be considered successful.

## JANUARY 2027 — WEEKS 12–13
### First strategic review

Evaluate:

- Market interest.
- Product-market fit.
- Dealer requirements.
- Pricing and margins.
- Distribution model.
- Marketing effectiveness.
- Time investment.
- Learning outcomes.
- Potential revenue.
- Opportunities for further expansion.

Decide whether to:
- Continue the Danish pilot.
- Expand outreach.
- Invest in more marketing materials.
- Explore Sweden.
- Formalize a regional representation agreement.
- Pause or change the approach.
