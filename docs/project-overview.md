# Project overview

Equator Scandinavia explores a Denmark-first market entry for Equator Cycling. Start date: October 12, 2026. This is a side project with an exploratory first three months.

User-supplied background, not independently verified: Equator is a small Czech bicycle company with roughly 16 years of experience, selling around 20–25 titanium frames/framesets and 300–600 wheels annually. Frames are designed in the Czech Republic and manufactured by specialist suppliers in China. Equator owns product development, supplier relationships, stock and fulfillment. No Scandinavian distribution network is established.

The Copenhagen-based project owner brings product management, UX/design, photography, filmmaking, marketing, cycling and software experience. Success includes learning, meaningful partner relationships, clear dealer requirements and evidence for the next decision. A first sale is welcome but not required.

Geography: Copenhagen and Greater Copenhagen, then Denmark. Sweden and Norway are later options. All outreach targets and relationship counts are exploratory planning targets, never guarantees.
