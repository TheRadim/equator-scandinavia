Privacy policy

This website is run by Equator Scandinavia, the Scandinavian representative of Equator Cycling. Contact: use the form on our contact page.

When you send a message or an order inquiry we receive your name, email address, phone number (if given), your message and the products in your bag. We use this only to answer you and to arrange your order with Equator Cycling, who manufacture the products.

Messages are delivered to us by a form-handling service acting on our behalf. We don't sell your details or use them for marketing without asking you first.

Visits are counted anonymously with Umami (no cookies, no personal data): pages viewed, country, referring website, device type and visit length.

Your bag and saved bike configurations are stored in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us until you submit an inquiry. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to us through the contact page.
