About Equator

A small family company in Zlín, Czech Republic, designing titanium bikes and carbon wheels.

Equator started with carbon wheels. Titanium frames were the logical next step: bikes you keep for years, not just a few seasons.

Tested, then tested again.

These aren't off-the-shelf frames with a new sticker. Every Equator frame is designed in-house: its own designer works out every angle, tube length, diameter and profile.

Each geometry is then refined over several prototypes, ridden on a test track in the Ore Mountains. Supple and comfortable where it should be. Stiff and direct where it counts.

Built to keep, winter after winter.

Titanium doesn't corrode and doesn't change its properties, so the frames need no paint. Rain, grit and salted winter roads won't make it rust. Scratches become part of the bike's story.

The wheels follow the same thinking: laced and tested by hand, with Sapim CX-Ray spokes and Enduro sealed bearings.

Now in Scandinavia.

Equator Scandinavia brings these bikes and wheels to riders and shops in Denmark, Sweden and Norway, starting in Copenhagen. Every request is answered personally.
