About Equator

Czech roots.
Northern horizons.

A small family company from Zlín, building titanium bicycles and carbon wheels for riders who plan to keep them for a long time.

Who we are

Equator Cycling is a Czech cycling company based in Zlín. It describes itself as a small family business, one that believes cycling, like business, is about relationships.

Equator started with carbon wheels. Titanium frames followed as what the company calls a logical next step. Today the range covers titanium road and gravel bicycles, framesets and carbon wheelsets.

How Equator designs

Every frame is designed and tested across several prototypes, with a clear goal for how it should ride. Equator describes its bicycles as designed, tested and assembled in Czechia, using parts sourced from specialist suppliers around the world.

The aim is a balance of performance, ride feel and durability, and the company says it learns from every frame it sells and every rider who gives feedback.

Why titanium

Titanium is chosen for riders who value durability and long-term ownership. It resists corrosion, so the frames can be left with a brushed, unpainted finish that wears its miles quietly.

How a bicycle actually rides depends on its geometry, tubing, tyres and build, not on material alone. That's why each Equator family has its own purpose: Sensei for road and gravel, Raiden for the road, and Yasei for longer adventures.

A different scale

Equator is not a mass-market manufacturer. The range is deliberately small, and the conversation is direct: you speak with people who know the bikes, not a call centre.

Equator Scandinavia

Equator Scandinavia is the regional representation for Denmark, Sweden and Norway, starting in Copenhagen. We help riders and shops discover Equator, configure the right bicycle and connect with the manufacturer.

Every build request is reviewed personally. We confirm price, availability and delivery with Equator before anything is paid.
