The people behind Equator

A small company.
A personal approach.

Equator is a Czech cycling company working with titanium frames and carbon wheels. Its bicycles are designed, tested and assembled in Czechia, with parts sourced from specialist suppliers worldwide.

Equator Scandinavia brings these bikes to riders in Denmark, Sweden and Norway, with a personal conversation about every build.
