    Sensei Gen 4

    Fast gravel, long days.

    Sensei Gen 4 is Equator's gravel bike for riders who move between tarmac and trail without changing bikes. Room for 52 mm tyres, a full set of mounts and clean internal routing, wrapped in a titanium frame of 1,850 g.

    Tyre clearance
    52 mm
    Frame weight (M)
    1,850 g
    Complete bike (M)
    9.4 kg
    Threaded BB
    T47

    Room for 52 mm tyres

    Clearance for tyres up to 52 mm wide, so the same bike runs fast slicks on the road and big volume rubber on rough gravel.

    Titanium, built to last

    Seamless 3Al-2.5V aerospace-grade titanium tubing. It doesn't rust, needs no paint and shrugs off scratches. A frame you keep for decades, not seasons.

    Clean, fully internal routing

    Cables run inside the head tube and chainstays for 1x and 2x groupsets, with a port for Di2 and internal routing for a dropper post.

    Threaded T47 bottom bracket

    A threaded T47 shell: quiet, simple to service with standard tools, and wide enough to route cables inside the frame.

    SRAM UDH, T-Type ready

    The Universal Derailleur Hanger is replaceable almost anywhere in the world and works with SRAM's hangerless T-Type Transmission.

    Mounts for the long way round

    Seat tube and twin down-tube bottle mounts, three top-tube mounts for a bag, plus rack and fender mounts.
