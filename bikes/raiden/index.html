    Raiden

    Pure road. Pure titanium.

    Raiden is Equator's dedicated road bike: a titanium frame with fully internal routing, room for 38 mm tyres and a threaded T47 bottom bracket. The SRAM Red AXS build weighs 8.4 kg in size L.

    Titanium, built to last

    Seamless 3Al-2.5V aerospace-grade titanium tubing. It doesn't rust, needs no paint and shrugs off scratches. A frame you keep for decades, not seasons.

    Fully internal routing

    Hoses and cables disappear into the frame for a clean front end and an uncluttered titanium silhouette.

    Threaded T47 bottom bracket

    A threaded T47 shell: quiet, simple to service with standard tools, and wide enough to route cables inside the frame.

    Room for 38 mm tyres

    Modern road clearance for tyres up to 38 mm, for more comfort and grip on rough roads and long days.

    SRAM UDH, T-Type ready

    The Universal Derailleur Hanger is replaceable almost anywhere in the world and works with SRAM's hangerless T-Type Transmission.
