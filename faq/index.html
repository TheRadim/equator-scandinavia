FAQ

Questions & answers

How do I order?
Configure your bike or choose your parts, add them to your bag and send an inquiry. We confirm price, availability and delivery in writing, and you order once you're happy.

Do I pay online?
No. Payment is arranged after you have accepted your order confirmation.

Can I change components?
The configurator shows Equator's factory builds. Mention any changes in your message and we'll check compatibility and price with Equator.

Which size do I need?
Tell us your height and inseam in your message and we'll help you choose.

Are you a shop?
We are the Scandinavian representative of Equator Cycling. Interested in selling Equator in your shop? Get in touch through the contact page.
